Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Eclipse is made by one person, tatnat. This page explains what the website and the Eclipse Launcher do with your data. The short version: Eclipse doesn't collect anything about you, and nothing is sent to us.

The website

  • There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your browser's local storage remembers a few things on your own device only: whether you've seen the intro, your Flappy Eclipse best score and your advancements. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and asks the GitHub API for the newest launcher version. Fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser loads from them; see GitHub's and Google's privacy policies.

The Eclipse Launcher and Eclipse Client

  • You sign in on Microsoft's own sign-in page. Your password never passes through Eclipse.
  • Your sign-in tokens, settings, instances and skins are stored only on your own PC, in the .tatnatclient folder.
  • To work, the launcher talks directly to Microsoft and Mojang (sign-in, game files and skins), Fabric, Forge and NeoForge (mod loaders), Modrinth, CurseForge and PacksMC (when you search for or install mods, packs, shaders or modpacks), mc-heads.net (player head pictures), Google Fonts (the launcher's fonts) and GitHub (launcher updates). Each of them has its own privacy policy.
  • Eclipse has no servers of its own and doesn't send any data about you or your PC to us.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
